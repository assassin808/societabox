# Capability gaps and who to contact (draft, 2026-09-28)

## What we have
- Research core: programmable cognitive bias (CHI 2026), LLM text world engines (Love First, Know Later), IRT-based curriculum ideas.
- Early funded use: IRIDeS tsunami evacuation; research teams at more than 10 universities.
- A front-end prototype (the town demo, hand-set rules).

## Gaps, in priority order
1. **Real human behavioural data and a validation pipeline.** This is the biggest gap. Without it there is no credibility claim for customers or VCs.
2. **Domain and go-to-market knowledge in market research.** We need a design-partner customer and someone who knows how research agencies buy and run studies.
3. **Validation science.** Psychometrics and survey methodology, to design fidelity metrics buyers trust.
4. **Engineering at scale.** Serving many persona or steering variants cheaply (multi-LoRA, vLLM), simulation orchestration, cost control.
5. **Product and design.** Turning the demo into a tool a researcher uses weekly.
6. **Compliance.** IRB and ethics for human studies, data licensing, careful claims about synthetic data.
