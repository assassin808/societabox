## Artificial Societies Benchmark (arXiv 2609.30030, Sep 2026)
- 11 tests across internal, construct, and external validity; 20 human sources; 9 LLMs. Code is public on GitHub (artificial-societies/artificial-societies-benchmark).
- Findings: synthetic surveys can match the average while getting wrong how people differ, how their answers correlate, and how they respond to changed conditions. Models answer too consistently, compress response scales, and distort relationships between traits. Richer respondent profiles help some models and hurt others. Fidelity in one domain does not carry over to others.
- Scope: survey-style, static, diagnostic (a scorecard). It does not propose a fix, and it does not cover interacting populations over time.
- Only the abstract was read; the full paper was blocked from the sandbox.
