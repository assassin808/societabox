One layer deeper

Others model who people are. We model how they decide.

Who people are, what they said and what they did are correlations: right on average, wrong at the moment that matters. How people decide is the driver you can change, so it answers questions about things that have never happened.

Method

Every trait is measured against real human studies.

  1. Pick a trait

    Following the crowd, framing, loss aversion, trust, patience.

  2. Set a level

    On a 0–4 scale, fitted to your customers’ past decisions.

  3. Measure it

    In validated classic experiments, such as Asch’s line test.

  4. Tune until it holds

    Through the prompt or, where the model allows, its internal state.

>0.96

Tuned on one experiment, it carried to a new one.

We set framing on an insurance choice. On a dilemma it never saw, the effect rose in step, in all 10 personas.
