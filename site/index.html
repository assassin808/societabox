The problem

Synthetic respondents look plausible. On our benchmark, today’s AI personas miss real human behaviour 83% of the time.

Big decisions are bets on people, and you get one take. Consultants are slow, surveys capture what people say rather than do, and simulators are fast but not human.

How it works

Others model who people are. We model how they decide.

Demographics, surveys and past behaviour are correlations. Decision traits are the driver you can change, and every trait is measured against classic human studies.

  1. Pick a trait

    Following the crowd, framing, loss aversion, trust.

  2. Set a target

    A level on a 0–4 scale, fitted to your customers’ past decisions.

  3. Measure it

    In classic human studies, such as Asch's line test.

  4. Tune until it holds

    Through the prompt or the model's inner state, on every model.

>0.96

We fit your customers’ traits from past decisions, run each option and stress-test the people. You see risk and upside by segment, and the trait driving each result.
