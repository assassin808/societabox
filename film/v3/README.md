1. **Launch.** "A launch" was drawn as a rocket. It is now an opened product box with a red NEW! sticker, labelled "a product launch".
2. **Today's methods.** A new scene before "one layer deeper" shows how teams guess today: surveys, focus groups, and a demographic persona. The persona's tags (age 23, grad student, Vancouver, likes oat milk) are written on one by one and stay on screen. They are struck out 0.45 s apart instead of 0.28 s.

   | Level | What she says | Reading |

   | 3.8 | "Whatever you're all having!" | always follows |
